# Privacy Policy — Unhook for LinkedIn

_Last updated: 14 June 2026_

Unhook for LinkedIn ("the extension") is designed to respect your privacy
completely. The short version: **the extension collects nothing, sends nothing,
and shares nothing.**

## What data the extension collects

**None.** Unhook for LinkedIn does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any
personal data or browsing activity.

## What data is stored, and where

Your preferences — which sections of LinkedIn you choose to hide, your redirect
choices, and your theme — are stored **locally on your own device** using the
browser's built-in extension storage. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is never transmitted to the developer or any third party,
- is not shared with or sold to anyone,
- can be removed at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## Network requests

The extension makes **no network requests**. It contains no analytics, no
tracking, no telemetry, and no remote code. It does not read, log, or send your
LinkedIn content anywhere. It works entirely by hiding page elements with CSS
and, optionally, redirecting the LinkedIn feed page to another LinkedIn page
that you select.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** — to save your preferences locally on your device.
- **Host access to `linkedin.com`** — so the extension can hide elements and
  apply your redirect preferences on LinkedIn pages. The extension runs only on
  LinkedIn and on no other websites.

## Third parties

There are no third-party services, SDKs, or trackers in this extension. The
"Donate" and "Support" links in the extension simply open external pages
(Ko-fi, your email client) in a new tab when you click them; no data is sent
automatically.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
location with a new "Last updated" date.
